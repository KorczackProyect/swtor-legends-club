Make Legends Canon

A local club for fans of SWTOR, Star Wars Legends and the Old Republic era.

Our Mission

We meet every week to discuss Star Wars Legends, SWTOR class stories, companions, Sith history, Jedi conflicts and the deeper lore of the Old Republic.

Club Activities

Lore Nights

Weekly discussions about SWTOR campaigns, ancient Sith and Jedi history.

Game Sessions

Group sessions to replay class stories and explore the Old Republic together.

Community Debates

Friendly debates about canon, Legends, companions and major story choices.

Join the Club
